### Other Resources  
* [Net Art Anthology](https://anthology.rhizome.org/) - retelling the history of net art from the 1980s to the 2010s!    
is an online (& offline) exhibition created by Rhizome, a non-profit organisation dedicated to new media art. The website presents 100 works of net.art history. The exhibition aims to address the shortage of historical perspectives on a field in which even the most prominent artworks are often inaccessible.    
* [WEB WORK: A HISTORY OF NET ART](https://www.artforum.com/print/200005/web-work-a-history-of-internet-art-465) By Rachel Greene (Article for Artforum magazine, May 2000)
