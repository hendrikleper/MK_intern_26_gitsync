# BERLIN 🐻🇩🇪
#### 10 to 14/11/2025

During an inspiring four-day city trip to Berlin, we immerse ourselves in the vibrant heart of the art scene. We visit an Erasmus+ partner institute, leading museums, private collections, meet artists in their studios, and let ourselves be surprised by artist-run spaces and alternative art initiatives. And who knows, we might end the day in a Berlin club. This trip offers a valuable opportunity to immerse ourselves in Berlin’s creative energy and connect with its diverse cultural network.

## Transport locally
We will visit places which are far apart. You are free to choose how you travel from one location to another, but keep in mind that Berlin is a large city.    
We recommend a public transport pass valid for 7 days covering the whole city center (zone A) and the suburbs (zone B) for €44,60.    
Buy your tickets [here](https://www.bvg.de/en/subscriptions-and-tickets/all-tickets).    
[Tickets, fare zones, and route maps of Berlin public transport](https://www.berlin.de/en/public-transportation/1772016-2913840-tickets-fares-and-route-maps.en.html) & 
more [info about public transport at night](https://www.berlin.de/en/public-transportation/1859225-2913840-night-buses-public-transport-at-night-.en.html).    
Another good option is to rent a bike with a service like [nextbike](https://www.nextbike.de/berlin/en/): around €1.50 for 15 minutes or €15 for 24 hours. Keep in mind that Berlin is huge! Some trips easily take you half an hour cycling (or 7km).

### School exchange
We’ll visit the [Weißensee Kunsthochschule](https://kh-berlin.de/en/), where [Pauline Doutreluingne](https://paulinedoutreluingne.com/), guest lecturer in the master’s program Raumstrategien / Spatial Strategies, will give us a guided tour. Afterwards, we’ll have an exchange with a group of her students, taking the form of a collective game.    

That game [Spit City](https://2023.transmediale.de/en/artwork/spit-city) by [Elio J Carranza](https://eliojcarranza.hotglue.me/) is an experimental pen-and-paper role-playing game in which players collaboratively create a fictional world and identity. From a queer, trans*feminist, and crip perspective, the game explores alternative ecologies and social structures. It encourages collective imagination and results in a hand-drawn map that reflects the players’ choices. Spit City is accessible, adaptable, and offers a playful, reflective way to engage in conversations about identity, community, and coexistence.

### Artist run initiatives & spaces
- [Kwia](https://kwia.berlin/) is a cozy, queer‑friendly ambient listening bar, modeled after Japanese "jazz kissa," where guests take off their shoes and relax on cushions, enjoying intimate electronic and experimental music through a high‑fidelity sound system in a living‑room-style space. Check [Insta](https://www.instagram.com/kwia.berlin/) for program.
- [Anorak](https://anorakanorak.com/) is a democratically run non-profit contemporary art association in Berlin, sustained by its members whose support enables an experimental artistic program, a community space, fair artist compensation, and the continuation of Anorak’s activities.
- [Spore initiative](https://spore-initiative.org/en/) is a cultural initiative that fosters climate justice, ecological regeneration, and education through collaborative projects and dialogue between earth protectors in the Global South and North, centered around the Spore House in Berlin as a hub for exchange, mutual learning, and the cultivation of shared values across diverse communities.
- [NEW FEARS](https://www.newfears.net/) is a Berlin project space founded in 2020 by July Weber that serves as a hybrid gallery and social platform at the intersection of choreography and visual arts, supporting experimental, body-focused performance, community events, and interdisciplinary exchange. Check [insta](https://www.instagram.com/newfears_gallery/) for program.
- [ACUD MACHT NEU](https://acudmachtneu.de/) operates three spaces at Kunsthaus ACUD: the Club, Studio and Gallery. The aim is to offer room for discourse, exchange and experimental artistic formats. 

### Art Institutes, Musea, Galleries, ... 
- [MONOM](https://www.monomsound.com/) is a spatial sound studio and performance venue in Berlin, located at Funkhaus, renowned for its immersive 4DSOUND system that enables artists and audiences to experience sound in three-dimensional space.
- The [Julia Stoschek Foundation](https://jsfoundation.art/) is a private contemporary art collection in Düsseldorf and Berlin, focused on time-based media art such as video, performance, and digital works, supporting both established and emerging artists. Current show is [MARK LECKEY: ENTER THRU MEDIEVAL WOUNDS](https://jsfoundation.art/exhibitions/mark-leckey-enter-thru-medieval-wounds). Free admission fro students.
- The [Boros Collection](https://boros-collection.com/) is a private collection of contemporary art housed in a former World War II bunker in Berlin. The collection features installations, sculptures, and photography by international artists such as Olafur Eliasson, Ai Weiwei, and Wolfgang Tillmans. The striking interplay between the bunker’s raw architecture and the contemporary artworks makes it one of the leading venues in Berlin’s art scene.
- [Hamburger Bahnhof](https://www.smb.museum/museen-einrichtungen/hamburger-bahnhof/home/) with [Petrit Halilaj](https://www.smb.museum/en/museums-institutions/hamburger-bahnhof/exhibitions/detail/petrit-halilaj/) & [Annika Kahrs](https://www.smb.museum/en/museums-institutions/hamburger-bahnhof/exhibitions/detail/annika-kahrs/).   
Petrit Halilaj (b. 1986, Kosovo) stages immersive, large-scale installations and his first opera that negotiate collective dreaming and the emancipatory power of art, notably in his 2025–26 Berlin solo show “An Opera Out of Time”.    
Annika Kahrs (b. 1984, Achim, Germany) creates immersive video and sound installations that probe the boundaries of music, investigating how acoustic information functions within social, cultural and political structures.
- [KW Institute for Contemporary Art](https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/) fosters experimental and critical approaches to art and culture. [Holly Herndon & Mat Dryhurst Starmirror](https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/holly-herndon-and-mat-dryhurst/)
- [Sinema Transtopia](https://sinematranstopia.com/), a new kind of cinema 
- [Kunstraum Kreuzberg/Bethanien](https://kunstraumkreuzberg.de/) is an exhibition space for contemporary art with a focus on current social and cultural issues. expo: Louder, Taller, Uglier, Weirder – Learning from Weeds (open daily 10:00 – 20:00)
- [Gropius Bau](https://www.berlinerfestspiele.de/en/gropius-bau) is a historic neo-Renaissance exhibition venue, designed in 1881 by Martin Gropius. There are two shows on view:     
Diane Arbus: [Konstellationen](https://www.berlinerfestspiele.de/en/gropius-bau/programm/2025/ausstellungen/diane-arbus), presents 454 black-and-white photographs, many shown for the first time, arranged as a “constellation” that invites visitors to explore freely and form their own connections between the portraits.    
Ligia Lewis is a Berlin-based artist and choreographer known for her politically charged, interdisciplinary works exploring race, gender, and resistance. At Gropius Bau, her exhibition [I’M NOT HERE FORRRRR…](https://www.berlinerfestspiele.de/en/gropius-bau/programm/2025/ausstellungen/ligia-lewis) presents new and existing pieces combining performance, film, and installation. Central to the show is Wayward Chant, a live, evolving work staged in the atrium.
- [Haus der Kulturen der Welt](https://www.hkw.de/en/) is a leading space for interdisciplinary art and discourse.
The current exhibition, [Global Fascisms](https://www.hkw.de/programme/global-fascisms) explores the global rise of far-right ideologies. Featuring works by around 50 international artists, it examines fascism’s aesthetic and social mechanisms.
The show connects historical and contemporary contexts, from propaganda to digital radicalization.
- [HAU / Hebbel am Ufer](https://www.hebbel-am-ufer.de/en/) is a renowned contemporary theater and performance venue in Berlin known for its experimental, interdisciplinary, and politically engaged productions. From 12 to 23/11 they host a Retrospective by choreographer Jefta van Dinther titled [A Queer Divine Dissatisfaction](https://www.hebbel-am-ufer.de/en/programme/festivals-projects/a-queer-divine-dissatisfaction) with [The Moving Image – A Series of Video Works](https://www.hebbel-am-ufer.de/en/programme/pdetail/jefta-van-dinther-the-moving-image-a-series-of-video-works) for free at HAU2.
