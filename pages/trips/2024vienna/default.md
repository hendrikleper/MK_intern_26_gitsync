# 🇦🇹 VIENNA 🇦🇹
#### 16-20 March 2024

Vienna seamlessly blends its rich historical legacy with a dynamic contemporary art scene, featuring classical art museums alongside vibrant hubs for modern expression, countercultural movements, and artist-run spaces. Our itinerary includes visits to renowned museums and art colleges, as well as exploration of lesser-known initiatives.     
Leveraging her year-long residency in Vienna, Rebecca Jane Arthur brings invaluable insight to this trip.     
A smaller group of students will collaborate with students from Technicum Wien on a second workshop with Patrick Loan, an artist and English teacher.
